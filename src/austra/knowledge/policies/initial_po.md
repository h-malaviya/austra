For a verified, manager-approved supplier, the agent may consider automatic PO approval for no more than 3 metric tonnes at a material unit price no greater than USD 7,000 per metric tonne. The corresponding material line value is at most USD 21,000. The quantity cap applies to the whole purchase requirement; splitting it across POs does not change the cap. The agent must use a current supplier quote and a matching material description. This policy does not yet define the approved supplier list, required grade, acceptable freight or tax charges, delivery terms, payment terms, or what constitutes an unusual price change. If any of those facts is missing, conflicting, or outside the stated caps, send the case to a manager. This policy never authorizes supplier bank-detail changes, payments, or customer delivery promises.
